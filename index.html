⚡ Fill Form

On the target website: click "Copy data for filling" above, open the page with the form, click the Fill Form bookmark, paste (Ctrl/Cmd+V) into the small panel that appears, then hit Fill form. It matches your keys against each field's id, name, placeholder, or label — then fills, checks, or selects accordingly and dispatches the right events so React/Vue-based forms pick it up too. Fields it can't match are listed so you can finish those by hand.
